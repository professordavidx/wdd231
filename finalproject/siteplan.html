Mythic Bestiary

Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Mythic Bestiary

This name was selected because a bestiary is traditionally a collection of legendary creatures and mythical beings. The title clearly communicates the purpose of the website while creating a mysterious and scholarly atmosphere.

Site Purpose

Mythic Bestiary is an informational website dedicated to mythical creatures from cultures around the world. Visitors can browse legendary beings, learn about their origins, abilities, and symbolism, and save favorite creatures for future reference.

Optional Domain Name

mythicbestiary.com (example)

Scenarios

  • What creatures appear in Japanese mythology and what powers are they known for?
  • Which mythical creatures are associated with protection, wisdom, or good fortune?
  • What is the difference between European dragons and Chinese dragons?
  • Which legendary creatures originate from Greek mythology?
