NP Membership Benefits

  • Standard directory listing across public indices.
  • Basic placement in cross-border non-profit listings.
  • Access to general networking assemblies.

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $250 / Year

  • Enhanced directory presence with custom tags.

Cost: $1,000 / Year

  • Full cross-border export operation auditing assistance.
  • VIP booth spaces assigned at the Tech Expo completely free.
